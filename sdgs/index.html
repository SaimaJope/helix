Helix and the goals

How we use them.

Every Helix project is mapped to the goals it serves before it starts, and judged against them when it ends. The goals give us a shared language with municipalities, funders and partners, and a way to check that many small projects add up to something larger.

Our five areas of work sit across the goals rather than inside any one of them: human development, knowledge and education, cooperation, innovation and sustainable futures.
