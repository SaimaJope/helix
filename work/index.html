Our Work

Five areas of work, one objective: the advancement of humanity and the common good. Here is what the institute focuses on, the projects that carry it, and what we publish along the way.

Areas of workProjectsPublications

What we do

We turn cooperation into coordinated, sustainable and meaningful action.

The institute's approach to advancing humanity is simple to say and hard to do: find the people who care about a problem, give them a way to work together, and stay long enough to learn something that can be passed on.

We do not run programmes at communities. We coordinate them with communities, connect them to knowledge and to each other, and make sure the work leaves behind capacity rather than dependence.
