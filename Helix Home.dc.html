About the institute

An international organization centered on sustainable human development — through cooperation, knowledge and innovation.

Helix Anthropis Institute promotes and facilitates initiatives that contribute to the advancement of humanity and the common good, without discrimination.

Working across disciplines and borders, the institute connects people, knowledge and resources in service of sustainable human development.
