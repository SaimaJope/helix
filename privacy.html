The short version

A minimal-data website by design.

Helix Anthropis Institute does not use analytics cookies, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, embedded social feeds or automated profiling on this website. Fonts and website scripts are served from the same website rather than fetched from third parties when a page opens.

TrackingNo analytics or advertising trackers
Browser storageOnly privacy, theme and cart preferences
Your controlClear visitor preferences at any time

There is no “accept all” button because there is nothing optional to accept. The public site uses only storage needed for features you request. You can inspect or clear it through .

Cookies & storage

What this browser remembers.

The public site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. It uses localStorage, a browser feature that keeps small values on your device. These values are not transmitted to Helix and cannot follow you across other websites.

NamePurposeWhen createdDuration
helix-privacyRecords that this version of the privacy notice was acknowledgedWhen you select “Understood”Notice shown again after 12 months or a material change
helix-themeRemembers light or dark display modeWhen you change the themeUntil cleared
helix-cartKeeps items and membership tier selected for the prototype shopWhen you use the cartUntil checkout or clearing the cart/preferences

This storage is used only when necessary to provide a feature you request or remember the privacy choice itself. You can remove it using or your browser controls. Blocking it may prevent the theme or cart from being remembered, but the rest of the site remains available.

Email and forms

Forms do not send data from the page.

Contact, volunteer and newsletter forms on this version of the website prepare a draft in your own email application. Nothing is uploaded when you type into the form. Data reaches Helix only if you choose to send that email.

Once sent, the message is processed in Helix’s email account to deal with the purpose shown above. You may withdraw newsletter consent at any time by replying or emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Shop prototype

No live order or payment processing yet.

The current checkout is a design prototype. Information typed there stays in the page for the current browser session and is not transmitted to Helix, a payment service or a delivery company. The page does not request card details and no payment or binding order is created.

Before live commerce is enabled, this notice must be updated with the actual payment, fulfilment, tax and accounting providers, lawful bases, retention periods and any international transfers. A live checkout must not be launched under this prototype notice.

Recipients and transfers

Who may handle data.

Website hosting

The public site is hosted with GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (part of Microsoft). GitHub receives the technical request data needed to deliver and protect the service. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement and subprocessor list.

Email

Messages sent to the published Helix address are handled through Google’s email infrastructure. Google may act as a service provider for account operation, security and storage. Read Google’s Privacy Policy.

External websites

Social networks and other external sites are not embedded. They receive data from your browser only after you choose to follow a link, and their own privacy terms then apply.

Outside the EEA

GitHub/Microsoft and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, the provider and Helix must rely on a lawful transfer mechanism such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. Provider notices describe their current locations and mechanisms. You can ask Helix for information relevant to your data.

Helix does not sell personal data. Data may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Your GDPR rights

You remain in control.

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Send a request to helixanthropisinstitute@gmail.com. Helix may ask for information needed to verify identity and will normally respond within one month. Exercising a right is generally free of charge.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to Luxembourg’s supervisory authority, the National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD). You may also contact the authority where you live or work.

Security, changes and contact

Questions are welcome.

Helix uses data minimisation, limited access and appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to the information handled. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

This notice will be reviewed when the website, providers or activities change. Material changes will reset the privacy acknowledgement so the notice appears again.
